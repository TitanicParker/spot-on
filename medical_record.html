The Clinical Record

A public reading copy of the scoped canonical evidential record underlying the repository. It preserves the clinical and governance sequence while removing direct patient identifiers that are not necessary to understand the case.

How to read this. The record distinguishes contemporaneous source text, patient reports, clinician interpretation, later institutional responses and retrospective evidence. Later facts should not be projected backwards. Source-status notes are part of the evidential discipline, not decoration. Underlying originals control where the canonical record says so.
