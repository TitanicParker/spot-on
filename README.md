# Spot On

**A source-governed reconstruction of one clinical history.**

This repository is built to answer a narrow question clearly:

> **What happened after the patient told the responsible neurologist, before discharge and while corrective options remained, that the problem which had brought him into care had not been adequately addressed?**

The repository does **not** begin with the finished discharge synthesis and ask the patient to disprove it. It begins earlier, reconstructs the open clinical state, then examines how that state became an authoritative travelling record and what happened when later evidence pressed against it.

## The case in one sequence

**open presenting problem → specialist observations → provisional allocation → pre-discharge Protest → remaining opportunity to act → clinical response/non-response → documentary finalisation → downstream inheritance → boundary-crossing evidence → local adaptation versus global reconstruction → governance → external reopening → PRKN/PARK2 with dystonia**

The case is therefore not mainly:

- a claim that Parkinsonism was missed — it was recognised;
- a claim that all foot symptoms were dystonia — genuine structural foot disease existed;
- a simple delayed-levodopa case — treatment timing is relevant but not the founding issue;
- or a hindsight claim that 2017 clinicians should have known the later molecular answer.

The central working hypothesis is narrower:

> **A still-contestable relationship may have acquired greater documentary definiteness and downstream authority than the demonstrated post-notice clinical process had earned.**

That hypothesis is deliberately falsifiable.

## One history, not two

There are not two competing histories, one belonging to the patient and one to the institution.

There is one history. The patient says he made the Protest. The responsible clinician participated in the encounter. Subsequent clinical and documentary decisions occurred after it. The patient then continued living with the same condition and with the knowledge that he had raised the unresolved problem.

What can diverge is **representation**.

The travelling record may omit a material event and later professional action may proceed through that incomplete representation. The omission does not create a second history and does not require the patient to forget what happened.

> **A patient cannot be required to adopt the omissions of his medical record as omissions from his own life.**

## The documentary phase change

The July 2017 discharge synthesis is central because it changed the evidential environment.

Before it, the case was an open information state: painful and structurally abnormal feet, abnormal gait, escalating neurological signs, severe young-onset Parkinsonism and a directly observed dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenon.

After it, later clinicians inherited a specialist-authored model in which the Parkinsonian branch had a rich neurological and genetic future while the foot territory could travel through structural, podiatric and possible peripheral-neuropathy routes.

The question is not whether those possibilities were medically discussable. It is whether the relationship between them was clinically resolved, adequately qualified, or expressly preserved as uncertain before the final account acquired authority.

## Why later records are read differently

After the phase change, repetition is not automatically independent corroboration.

A later note may contain:

- a genuinely new observation;
- an inherited proposition;
- repetition of inherited history;
- new evidence interpreted through the inherited model;
- or a genuine reopening of the founding allocation.

Later events are therefore used mainly as **stress tests**. The repository asks what happened when new evidence crossed the boundaries created in 2017.

Important examples include the procyclidine withdrawal/rechallenge pattern, later neurological reclassification of a foot-drop-like phenomenon, morning warm-up, OFF-related pain and dystonia, dopaminergic relief, external movement-disorders review and eventual PRKN/PARK2 confirmation with dystonia.

None of those facts is used as a time machine. Their value is to test **corrigibility**.

## The levodopa asymmetry

The 2017 synthesis documents the decision to delay levodopa in unusually careful prospective terms: rationale, risk, strategy, patient understanding and even possible future regret.

That may be entirely responsible documentation.

Its importance is comparative. The surviving record is much less transparent about the post-notice clinical work by which the original foot problem was separated from a directly observed dystonic-looking lower-limb phenomenon.

A later allegation that levodopa should have been started earlier therefore meets a ready-made defence. The more prior question remains:

> **What happened after direct notice, before closure, that justified the authority later given to the foot/peripheral allocation?**

Even a completely justified decision to delay levodopa does not answer that question.

## Function before motive

This repository separates documentary effect from mental-state allegations.

It first asks what travelled, what did not, what later evidence was allowed to change, what governance actually reviewed, and whether the originating clinician was being treated as though he were merely another downstream reader of his own record.

Only after those matters are established should questions of knowledge, appreciation, intention, concealment or falsification be considered.

**Poor reasoning is not dishonesty. Omission is not automatically concealment. Inaccuracy is not automatically falsification.**

Native production history — dictation, drafts, revision timestamps, audit data and source material for patient-attributed language — is therefore especially important to any stronger provenance or mens rea question.

## What could defeat the theory

The repository should narrow or change if evidence shows, for example:

- a targeted post-Protest reassessment;
- contemporaneous reasoning establishing the disputed relationship before closure;
- independent pre-notice support for the strongest patient-attributed separation language;
- explicit preservation of the patient's unresolved concern;
- later genuine reconstruction of the founding allocation;
- an independently reconstructed governance review based on primary evidence;
- or expert evidence that no further relational inquiry was reasonably required.

A framework about corrigibility must itself be corrigible.

## Status and limits

This is a personal analytical reading project built around clinical, documentary, ethical, governance and legal questions.

The **source record controls facts**. Analytical pages control only their stated inferences. Later evidence is not projected backwards as knowledge available in 2017. Mixed mechanisms remain possible. Legal breach, causation and damages require appropriately instructed independent expert and legal assessment.

The pre-discharge Protest is treated as a fixed premise for the repository's personal reconstruction. Any external court, regulator or adjudicative process may impose separate proof requirements.
