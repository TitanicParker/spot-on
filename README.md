## The governing question

> **What happened to corrective information after it was received, and what was later allowed to change the authority of the decision made after it?**

The case is not principally a claim that Parkinsonism was missed, that every foot symptom was dystonia, or that later genetics should have been known in 2017. The central working hypothesis is narrower:

> **A still-contestable relationship may have acquired greater documentary definiteness and downstream authority than the demonstrated post-notice clinical process had earned.**

The repository is structured to test that proposition rather than assume it.

## One history, documentary overlay

There are not two competing histories. There is one history. The patient says he made the pre-discharge Protest; the responsible clinician participated in that encounter; subsequent clinical and documentary decisions occurred after it; and the patient continued living with knowledge of what had occurred.

What can diverge is representation.

> **A patient cannot be required to adopt the omissions of his medical record as omissions from his own life.**

## Status and limits

The source record controls facts. Analytical pages control only their stated inferences. Later evidence is not projected backwards as knowledge available in 2017. Mixed mechanisms remain possible. Legal breach, causation and damages require appropriately instructed independent expert and legal assessment.
